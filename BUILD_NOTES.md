# Product strategy and build notes

## Product position

**Core promise:** IB Maths, explained properly.

Mr Flynn IB should feel like an expert education brand, not a generic tutor directory. Rob’s personal explanations are the product advantage. Authority should come from clear teaching, real evidence and IB-specific experience rather than inflated language.

## Initial audiences and routes

| Audience | Main need | Primary route |
|---|---|---|
| IB student | Understand topics and prepare for exams | Courses → Teachable |
| Parent | Choose appropriate support | Tutoring / course guidance |
| Teacher | Give students reliable supplementary explanations | Free resources / school licence |
| Head of Mathematics or IB Coordinator | Cohort access and procurement | School licence enquiry |
| Existing customer | Reach purchased lessons | Teachable login |
| Book reader | Learn more and continue into the platform | Book → resource or course |

## Brand system

- **Ink:** `#0B1530` — authority and depth
- **Teal:** `#16755F` — clarity, progress and primary actions
- **Gold:** `#E8AA2D` — emphasis and warmth
- **Paper:** `#FBFAF6` — calm, editorial background
- **Sky:** `#DCEEF4` — supportive secondary surfaces
- Typography uses a fast system sans stack, with restrained serif use inside the book visual and mathematics illustration.
- Visual style avoids stock-school clichés and uses editorial spacing, mathematical notation and purposeful product cards.

## Conversion hierarchy

1. Primary homepage action: **Find your course**
2. Secondary: **Explore free resources**
3. Audience-specific actions: tutoring application, school information request, book purchase
4. Existing-student utility: Teachable login

## Commercial and IP constraint

School and partnership arrangements should preserve Mr Flynn IB’s ownership of its brand, materials, name and image rights, customer data and direct customer relationship. Avoid broad exclusivity or arrangements that make the business appear to be merely supplying tutoring labour to another platform.
